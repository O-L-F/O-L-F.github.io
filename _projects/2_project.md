As most researchers may know, when Elon Musk bought out Twitter, it did not take long for him to put the API behind a paywall. 

Researchers now have to either face the high costs of paying to access this once free service, or finding an adequate work-around. 

My solution to this problem was to write a userscript that parses through the DOM on your twitter page and collects the tweets, their author, if they are a retweet and when they were posted. 

More information can be found on my GitHub. V2 is forthcoming. Please use with caution and with a burner account. Godspeed !
