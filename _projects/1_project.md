This project is attached to my work as an RA for [Prof. Maria Popova](https://www.popovaprof.ca/) of McGill and [Prof. Nikolay Marinov](https://nikolayvmarinov.github.io/) from University of Gothenburg. 

The goal was to [extend the original dataset](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13501763.2020.1744688) developed by [Prof. Arthur Dyevre](https://www.arthurdyevre.org/) and [Dr. Nicolas Lampach](https://www.law.kuleuven.be/pub/en/staff/00112618). You can find the [article here](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/13501763.2020.1744688), and the [original dataset here](https://euthority.eu/?page_id=795).

The dataset is completed now, spanning from 1961 to 2025 (inclusive). Please contact me by email for access, upload is forthcoming. In addition to expanding its timeline, the extension also features data at the [NUTS 3 level](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/nuts/overview).
